How to File a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you believe that content available on HOTT WRESTLING infringes one or more of your copyrights, please send a written notice containing all of the following five elements:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work — A description of the copyrighted work that you claim has been infringed. If multiple works are covered by a single notification, provide a representative list.
  2. Identification of the infringing material — A description of the material you claim is infringing and information reasonably sufficient to locate it on the Service (e.g., the URL or direct link to the content).
  3. Your contact information — Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address so we can reach you regarding your claim.
  4. Good faith statement — A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. Statement of accuracy under penalty of perjury — A statement that the information in the notification is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
