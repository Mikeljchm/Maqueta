Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate this service. This includes interaction data (likes and comments) submitted voluntarily by users, and anonymized analytics data about site usage.

2. User Interaction Data

When you interact with content on this site — including liking posts or leaving comments — that interaction data is stored in our database powered by Supabase (supabase.com). This data includes the content of your comment and a post identifier. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information in connection with these interactions unless you voluntarily provide it in a comment.

3. Analytics

We may use third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools may collect anonymized data such as pages visited, time spent on site, device type, and geographic region. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses browser local storage to remember your age verification status and your personal preferences such as saved posts. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

5. Payment Information

Payments are processed through PayPal and Binance Pay. We do not collect or store your payment card information. Please refer to PayPal's and Binance's respective privacy policies for information on how they handle your payment data.

6. Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies: Supabase (database and interaction storage), Netlify (hosting and analytics), Google Fonts (typography), Imgbox (image hosting), PayPal (payment processing), Binance Pay (cryptocurrency payment processing).

7. Data Retention

Interaction data (likes and comments) is retained as long as the associated content exists on the site. You may request deletion of your interaction data by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

This site is strictly for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect any information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that a minor has provided information, we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
